The experience

Made for slowing all the way down

"Peaceful Peaks is pure magic. The silhouettes of the trees against the nights sky, the beauty of the moon and the stars, the sunsets so incredible!"
— Colita & Joe

  • Dark-sky stargazing

    Some of the darkest skies in Colorado, plus a telescope to explore them.

  • We haul your gear

    Park below, stroll a lit quarter-mile path, and Joe brings your bags up on the four-wheeler.

  • Truly unplugged

    No Wi-Fi and no cell signal. Hammocks, books and games instead.
