Hosted by a Superhost · Boncarbo, Colorado

  • Lit pathways

    Low solar garden lights guide you to the outhouse without washing out the stars.

Cloudscape Retreat is our white canvas bell tent, a bright, airy space surrounded by ponderosa pines.

The bed is made, the rugs are down and the coffee maker is ready for the morning. Spend the day in a hammock, then pull a chair up to the fire pit for the sunset.

What this place offers

  • Beds made up with extra pillows
  • Powered lights
  • Percolating coffee maker
  • Grill and kitchen setup with utensils
  • Fire pit and propane fire pit
  • Camp chairs
  • Hammocks
  • Big cooler of drinking water
  • Propane heater on request
  • Books and games
  • Telescope for stargazing
  • Solar-lit paths
  • Clean, stocked outhouse
  • Bear spray provided
  • Pets and kids welcome
  • No Wi-Fi or cell signal
