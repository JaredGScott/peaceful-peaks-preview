Location

Getting to Peaceful Peaks

Boncarbo, Colorado · Las Animas County · about 6,700 ft

Off the Highway of Legends, west of Trinidad

Peaceful Peaks sits on a quiet ridge outside Boncarbo, about 15 miles from Trinidad and its restaurants and shops. We'll send your exact address and arrival directions with your booking confirmation.

Arriving

  • The last stretch is on a county road. Plan to arrive in daylight if you can.
  • Park at the lower parking area. The walk up to the sites is about a quarter mile, steep, lit and well marked, and takes most guests 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Joe hauls your gear up on the four-wheeler and trailer, so pack what you need and leave the heavy lifting to us.
  • There's no cell signal at camp, so download your confirmation and directions before you leave town.
