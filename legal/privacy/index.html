Privacy Policy

Last updated Effective date

This Privacy Policy explains how Legal business name, e.g. Peaceful Peaks Glamping LLC ("Peaceful Peaks", "we") collects and uses information when you visit this website or book a stay.

Information we collect

  • Booking requests: your name, email address, phone number, requested stay, dates, number of guests, whether pets are coming and any message you send us.
  • Reservations and payments: details needed to confirm and manage your stay. Card payments are handled by payment processor, which receives your card details directly; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Website data: basic technical information your browser sends (such as IP address and browser type), which our hosting provider may log for security. List any analytics tool, or state that none is used.

How we use it

  • To answer your request, confirm and manage your reservation, and contact you about your stay.
  • To keep our booking calendar accurate (we use Google Calendar to manage availability).
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • To send you occasional news or offers only if you ask us to; you can opt out at any time.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business (for example our email, calendar, hosting and payment providers), when required by law, or to protect the safety of guests and property.

Third-party content

Our location pages embed Google Maps, which may set cookies or collect data under Google's Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

We keep reservation records for as long as needed for the stay and for tax and accounting purposes (generally number years), and booking requests that don't become reservations for up to number months.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop sending you marketing emails, by emailing peacefulpeaksglamping@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information online.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with a new "last updated" date.
