Cancellation Policy

Last updated Effective date

We know plans change. This policy applies to reservations booked directly with Peaceful Peaks Glamping. Owners: confirm or change these tiers before launch.

Cancellations by guests

  • 14 or more days before check-in: full refund, minus any payment processing fees.
  • 7 to 13 days before check-in: 50% refund of the nightly rate; cleaning fees and taxes refunded in full.
  • Less than 7 days before check-in, or no-show: no refund of the nightly rate.
  • Early departure: unused nights are not refunded.

To cancel, email peacefulpeaksglamping@gmail.com. The cancellation date is the date we receive your email.

Changing your dates

We're happy to move your stay to new dates subject to availability. Requests made 7 or more days before check-in are free; later changes are treated as a cancellation.

Weather, fire and safety

Mountain weather is part of the adventure, and rain or cold alone is not a reason for a refund. If we cancel your stay because conditions are unsafe (for example wildfire, a road closure, or severe weather that makes the property inaccessible) you may choose new dates or a full refund.

Cancellations by us

In the rare event we must cancel, you will receive a full refund of everything you paid, and we will try to help you rebook.
